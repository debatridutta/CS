Offset: Production Schedule

Version 1.0 · 15 November 2026 · Draft for discussion

Offset is a psychological sci-fi thriller produced by Velvet Coconut Productions (Ireland) with Penned Pictures (Germany) and Ginga Stories (Spain). This document sets out the planned schedule from financing through to delivery and premiere. All dates are indicative and to be confirmed.

1. Key facts

Script98 pages, approximately 98 minutes. Draft dated 15 July 2026.
Shoot1 to 29 June 2027. 24 shooting days on six-day weeks.
UnitsThree units: Eifel (17 days), Cologne (3 days) and Barcelona (4 days).
Budget€1,200,000, approximately €28,000 per shoot day.
Post-production26 weeks with Ginga Stories, Spain. Post budget €201,700.
Delivery31 January 2028. Target premiere: Berlinale, February 2028.

Units and scenes

Eifel: Villa Glashütte, Utscheid. Days 1 to 17 (1 to 19 June).

  • Arrival across the estate, the façade, and the dawn walk down the lane.
  • The dining room, shot in story order so the feast rots on camera, through to the final session.
  • The living room, bedrooms, stairwell, kitchen, bathroom and the red-light party.
  • Fantasies: Cali's cake, Jacqueline's bath, the mother's dinner, Ezra's crowd, Flo's glass at the lake and Cai's forest.
  • Nights: Frans's hunt, the building on the hill, and Eve's rescue and fall.

Cologne: Ungers house. Days 18 to 20 (21 to 23 June).

  • House interiors (scene split to be confirmed with Penned Pictures).
  • Pool day: the black water and the green door, with cast in the water.

Barcelona: flashback unit, Ginga Stories. Days 21 to 24 (25 to 29 June).

  • The mother's flat: the bath, the dance, the pillow and her decline.
  • Cine Malda: the street, the cinema and the bathroom cubicle.

On set

Two RED Komodo cameras (in kind); practical LED neck devices; a VFX supervisor on the glow, sealed-mouth and water days; prosthetics; a crowd for Ezra's fantasy; one stunt jump (to be confirmed); and night work in the forest.

4. Pre-production, 12 April to 29 May 2027

Seven weeks, run from the Cologne production office.

  • Production office and German crew in Cologne (Penned Pictures).
  • Remaining cast, including the Spanish lead and the mother.
  • Device build: practical LED neck units and the black boxes.
  • Look development and camera tests, including green light on skin.
  • VFX shot planning: the glow, sealed mouths and the black water.
  • Prosthetics: the pregnancy, sealed mouths and Eve.
  • Location preparation, with exclusive use of Villa Glashütte.
  • Insurance placed, a song licence quote obtained and a sales agent attached.
