Units and scenes

Eifel · Villa Glashütte, UtscheidDays 1 – 17 · 1 – 19 June

  • Arrival across the estate, the façade, and the dawn walk down the lane.
  • The dining room, shot in story order so the feast rots on camera, through to the final session.
  • The living room, bedrooms, stairwell, kitchen, bathroom and the red-light party.
  • Fantasies: Cali's cake, Jacqueline's bath, the mother's dinner, Ezra's crowd, Flo's glass at the lake and Cai's forest.
  • Nights: Frans's hunt, the building on the hill, and Eve's rescue and fall.

Cologne · Ungers houseDays 18 – 20 · 21 – 23 June

  • House interiors, with Penned Pictures.
  • Pool day: the black water and the green door, with cast in the water.

Barcelona · flashback unit, Ginga StoriesDays 21 – 24 · 25 – 29 June

  • The mother's flat: the bath, the dance, the pillow and her decline.
  • Cine Malda: the street, the cinema and the bathroom cubicle.

On set

Two RED Komodo cameras (in kind); practical LED neck devices; a VFX supervisor on the glow, sealed-mouth and water days; prosthetics; a crowd for Ezra's fantasy; one stunt jump; and night work in the forest.

03Pre-production

22 March to 29 May 2027: ten weeks, run from the Cologne production office.

  • Production office and German crew in Cologne (Penned Pictures).
  • Remaining cast, including the Spanish lead and the mother.
  • Device build: practical LED neck units and the black boxes.
  • Look development and camera tests, including green light on skin.
  • VFX shot planning: the glow, sealed mouths and the black water.
  • Prosthetics: the pregnancy, sealed mouths and Eve.
  • Location preparation, with exclusive use of Villa Glashütte.
  • Insurance placed, a song licence quote obtained and a sales agent attached.
